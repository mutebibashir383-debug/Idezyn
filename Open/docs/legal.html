Privacy Policy

What Open processes. To perform diagnostics, Open reads information from a connected iPhone: model, product type, serial number, UDID, ECID, iOS version, activation state, passcode status and battery level. This stays on your computer.

What leaves your computer. Open contacts api.ipsw.me to look up device names and currently signed iOS versions, and downloads iOS firmware from the address that service provides. The installed app checks GitHub for updates. These requests do not include your serial number, UDID or ECID. Those third parties handle connection data (such as your IP address) under their own policies.

Backups. Backups are written only to the folder you choose and are never uploaded by Open.

Job history and shop details. Customer names, notes, job history and shop details you enter stay on your computer. You can export or delete them at any time.

Crash reports. When you ask Open to read crash reports, they are copied from the device into a temporary folder on your computer, analyzed locally and then deleted. The reports stay on the device and are never uploaded.

Firmware and support. Firmware is downloaded from Apple’s servers and checked against its checksum. If you choose to send details to support by WhatsApp or email, only the report you reviewed is shared, and device identifiers are included only if you tick the box.

Diagnostic reports and logs. Reports are saved only when you export them. The app keeps a local log of technical events (no device identifiers) to help with support; you can delete it at any time.

Sharing and sale. 5FYN does not sell your personal data and does not share device information with advertisers.
