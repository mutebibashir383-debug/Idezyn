Questions

Does it work on every iPhone?

It supports iPhones that Apple still signs iOS for, and guides you through Recovery Mode on every model from the original iPhone onward. New models can need an app update, which installs automatically.

Can it remove iCloud Activation Lock or an Apple ID?

No. If Find My iPhone was on, the iPhone still asks for the original Apple ID after a reset. This protects real owners, and no legitimate software can remove it.

Will I lose my data?

Exiting Recovery Mode and updating iOS keep data. Erase and restore deletes everything, and Open offers a backup first when the iPhone is unlocked.

Can it fix hardware problems?

No. Cracked screens, bad batteries, water damage and failing boards need a hardware repair.

Is it really free?

Yes. Open is free to download and use.
